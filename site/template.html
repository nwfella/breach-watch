  • Press & announcements — Canadian org watchlist + breach queries (EN/FR)

Why “no-dump” coverage matters

Have I Been Pwned can only alert when a stolen database is actually published. Many incidents — especially in Canada — never surface as dumps. We track the announcement: the day an organization or regulator discloses an incident, you see it here.

Canada reality check: neither the federal OPC nor Québec's CAI publishes a per-incident public register, so Canadian coverage leans on regulator news, court/company disclosures and press — which is exactly the gap this site fills.

Honest limits

  • Record counts are as reported by the source — “accounts affected”, not unique people.
  • “Canada”/“Québec” tags on news items come from the query context, not per-record verification.
  • We monitor disclosures; we are not the disclosing authority.
  • Same incident covered by several outlets may appear multiple times (entity resolution is on the roadmap).
